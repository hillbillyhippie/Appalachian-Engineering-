The Bucket

A safe harbor for honest talk. Pull up a chair and kick the can down the road.

Our Mission

The Bucket is a place for people to talk about what is really going on without fear of gossip, bullying, or judgment. It is for real life: relationship problems, family concerns, medical bills, harassment, rumor damage, difficult choices, worries, and anything else that feels too heavy to carry alone.

You can talk about relationship intimacy in a respectful, real-world way: wanting to feel seen, wanted, cared for, and connected; feeling dismissed or treated like a roommate; or wanting to show up better for your partner. For example, “My wife feels unseen, and I want to do better—what are some thoughtful ways to show her I care?” is welcome. This is about empathy, communication, affection, and connection—not explicit sexual details, positions, or techniques.

What comes in stays here. Share only what you choose to share, leave out names and identifying details, and give one another room to speak honestly. This is a community commitment, not a promise of absolute or legal confidentiality.

What Is Welcome

  • Honest questions and personal concerns.
  • Relationship, family, financial, medical, or work problems.
  • Respectful conversations about intimacy, trust, feeling valued, and reconnecting with a partner—without explicit sexual details.
  • Ideas, disagreements, worries, mistakes, and experiences you need to think through.
  • Respectful support, lived experience, and thoughtful disagreement.
  • Talking about harmful gossip and how it affects you or someone else.

What Is NOT Allowed

NO GOSSIP. NO RUMORS. NO BULLIES. NO MISOGYNY. NO HARASSMENT. NO EXPLICIT SEXUAL CONTENT OR UNWANTED SEXUAL COMMENTS.

  • Do not repeat personal stories, names, rumors, or identifying details outside the room.
  • No explicit sexual stories, positions, techniques, or sexualized banter. Respectful discussion of relationship intimacy and emotional connection is welcome.
  • No sexual pressure, unwanted sexual comments, or degrading talk about anyone.
  • Do not harass, threaten, intimidate, shame, manipulate, spam, or deliberately disrupt.
  • Disagree with ideas without attacking the person or digging up private business.
  • If you came only to gossip or stir trouble, please stay out.

When a Problem Gets Serious

The Bucket can be a place to put feelings into words and realize you are not alone. It is not emergency help or professional counseling. If anyone is in immediate danger or may hurt themselves or someone else, contact emergency or crisis services instead of waiting for an online response.

The Submission Box Is Not Open Yet

Nothing can be submitted through this page yet. This is a static website and it does not collect messages or store submissions.

Before the Bucket opens, the privacy details, retention rules, moderator process, and contact method must be made clear.
