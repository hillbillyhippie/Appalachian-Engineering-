The Bucket

Pull up a chair. Meet a neighbor. Toss in an idea.

A community room in the making

The Bucket is an idea for a welcoming place where Penns Valley neighbors can mill around, meet one another, share what’s on their minds, and talk about things that matter to the community—like an open house at the local hardware store.

The plan is to let people submit a thought, question, concern, or awkward situation for private review. The site owner can decide whether it’s suitable to bring into a conversation. Submissions would not appear automatically or be published as public posts.

The Bucket is Vegas, baby: what comes out of the bucket stays in the bucket.

Please respect people’s privacy: don’t repeat personal stories or identifying details outside the room, and only share what you’re comfortable sharing. No website can guarantee that other people will keep something confidential.

The submission box is not open yet

Nothing can be submitted through this page yet. This website is a simple static site and does not collect messages. Before opening the Bucket, a private form service needs to be selected and configured so submissions go to a review queue—not straight onto the website.

Before it opens, the privacy details, how long submissions are kept, and how to contact the moderator will be explained here. “Anonymous” would mean your name is not shown to other participants; the form or hosting service may still process technical information, so complete anonymity cannot be promised.

Honest talk, neighborly conduct

The goal is honest, respectful discussion about what concerns people, good ideas, and whatever else brings neighbors together. Nobody has to answer a prompt, share more than they want, or participate in role-play.

  • No harassment, threats, gossip, personal attacks, spam, or deliberate disruption.
  • Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
  • Leave out names and identifying or confidential details.
  • Prompts and submissions may be declined if they don’t fit the community guidelines.

If a submission form is added, submissions will be reviewed before anything is shared in a discussion. The moderator may decline or remove material that violates the guidelines.
