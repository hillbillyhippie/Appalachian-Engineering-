The Bucket

A place to toss in a thought, an idea, a question—or just say hello.

Pull up a chair

This is the start of a community conversation room for Penns Valley: a place for neighbors to share good ideas, talk about what matters to them, and have a respectful conversation. The spirit is local radio and open conversation, with a nod to WSOV 101.7.

This is an independent Appalachian Engineering project, not an official WSOV page or a station-affiliated forum.

Getting the conversation set up

Posting is not live yet. This website is currently a simple static site, so it cannot receive or publish messages on its own. Before visitors can post anonymously, we need to connect a forum or message-board service and decide how moderation, spam, privacy, and community guidelines will work.

For now, this page is the front door to that idea—not a working public forum. When the conversation space is ready, this room can link to it or host it here.

The kind of room we want

  • Share ideas and experiences; disagree without attacking people.
  • Don’t post private information about yourself or anyone else.
  • Keep it welcoming, useful, and neighborly.
