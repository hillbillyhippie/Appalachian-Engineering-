Welcome to Appalachian Engineering, the home of Hillbilly Hippie Hi-Fi and high fives. It's really a place where you can look at speakers, I'll build you a speaker, and we can talk about speakers. I love live music and cool merch, and festival season is always fun. Any kind of music is welcome here, and if you want to toss around some ideas—or just sit and bullshit and kick the can down the road—pull up a chair. You're in the right spot.

You can find my merchandise on Etsy and TeePublic. Just search for Appalachian Engineering or Hillbilly Hippie Hi-Fi. You can also keep up with Appalachian Engineering and Hillbilly Hippie Hi-Fi on Facebook.
